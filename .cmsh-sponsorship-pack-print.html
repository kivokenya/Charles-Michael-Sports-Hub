Mount Kenya International Taekwondo Open Tournament 2027

10 and 11 April 2027
Limuru, Kiambu County, Kenya

An opportunity to make a local impact

Charles Michael Sports Hub is a community sports organisation creating opportunities for children, young people and adults to train, compete and grow. Taekwondo is our flagship programme and the focus of our international tournament.

The Hub is also building an inclusive multi-sport offer across athletics, martial arts, boxing, football, darts and board games. Sponsorship helps us deliver a well-organised event, welcome athletes and families, and strengthen the community pathways that make participation possible.

3. Confirm in writing

Both parties review and sign a sponsorship agreement before any payment or activation.

Responsible partnership

We aim to provide sponsors with clear commitments, appropriate brand visibility and a post-event account of agreed activity. Sponsor logos and materials are shared for approval before publication or printing. Event plans, audience estimates and venue arrangements may be updated as details are confirmed.

Cash sponsorship is not a donation to an individual athlete. Contributions support the agreed event or programme activity described in the written agreement. No sponsor benefit is guaranteed until it has been confirmed in that agreement.
