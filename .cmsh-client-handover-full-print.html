01 · THE ORGANISATION & THE WEBSITE

A sports hub for the community

What the site represents

Charles Michael Sports Hub (CMSH) is presented as a Limuru-based community sports organisation creating opportunities for children, young people and adults to take part, develop skills and progress. Taekwondo is the established flagship. Athletics, martial arts, boxing, football, darts and board games are described as a developing wider offer, with availability to be confirmed.

Mission & values

Sport is framed as a pathway to discipline, confidence, respect, fitness and perseverance, as well as a route to competition. The site highlights inclusion, safety, integrity and community partnership.

Limuru · Kiambu County

Founding team

Michael Charles James Karuga is identified as founder: a 2nd Dan black belt with nine years of coaching experience and World Taekwondo Federation (WTF) and Kenya Taekwondo Federation (KTF) certificates.

Moses Kamau is a co-founder and taekwondo black belt. Ngugi Kinyanjui is co-founder and General Manager, with a focus on youth empowerment, programmes, organisational leadership and community development.

What visitors can do

Four practical journeys

Responsive layout for desktop and mobile

Explore programmes

  • Review junior taekwondo (ages 8–17) and adult training.
  • Learn about holiday camps, school partnerships and future coaching/referee development.
  • Ask about current schedules and fees; these are confirmed by the Hub.

Register interest in the Hub

  • Select one or more sports and enter contact and age-group details.
  • Minors are prompted for parent/guardian contact details.
  • The site prepares a WhatsApp message for review and sending to the Hub.

Send a tournament entry enquiry

  • Enter athlete, club, event and category details for Kyorugi and/or Poomsae.
  • Required fields adapt to the selected event and youth categories request guardian details.
  • The athlete or guardian opens WhatsApp, reviews and sends the enquiry.

Review sponsorship information

  • Compare Gold, Silver and Bronze packages, benefits and in-kind options.
  • Download the separate three-page sponsorship pack.
  • View five temporary logo spaces on the event page until sponsors are confirmed.

Event information on the site

Visitors can review event divisions, provisional schedule, registration guidance, spectator information, sponsor opportunities and the technical format. A Google Maps search link points to Limuru Constituency rather than a confirmed venue pin.

Technical and approval note: The Olympic-style wording describes the Kyorugi bracket format only. It does not claim Olympic or federation sanction. The event regulations still need to confirm the draw, byes, any repechage, medal bouts and applicable technical rules. Schedule and venue details remain subject to organiser confirmation.

03 · SPONSORSHIP & PLATFORM DELIVERY

Partnership proposition

Built to welcome supporters

The sponsor area connects the event to potential partners through clear package levels, recognition benefits and a downloadable pack. The five spaces beneath the tournament banner are temporary CMSH-logo placeholders and are expressly not confirmed sponsors.

TITLE SPONSOR

Gold · KSh 250,000

Title billing, prominent event branding, social media, venue exposure, VIP passes and post-event reporting.

OFFICIAL SPONSOR

Silver · KSh 150,000

Event banner/backdrop, event T-shirts, social announcements, booth space, VIP passes and event website visibility.

SUPPORTING SPONSOR

Bronze · KSh 75,000

Main banner, social announcement, VIP passes, programme acknowledgement and website visibility.

Bespoke and in-kind contributions are also invited. The online sponsorship pack includes planning estimates; attendance figures are estimates, not guarantees.

Platform & technical foundations

  • Astro-based static site; production build publishes files from dist.
  • Responsive layouts, branded navigation, mobile menu and contact shortcuts.
  • Page titles/descriptions, social-sharing metadata, organization/event structured data and sitemap.
  • Privacy, cookie, terms and safeguarding pages are part of the site.

Operational scope to understand

  • There is no admin dashboard, database or CMS for editing content.
  • Hub and athlete forms do not save entries, accept payments or receive document uploads.
  • The athlete form is an enquiry, not official entry confirmation; the organiser completes registration.
  • Club/team and officials are described on the event page, but do not have dedicated online submission flows.
The website is a public information and enquiry platform, not an event-management system. Content and enquiry follow-up remain the Hub's responsibility; significant updates require a code/content release.

04 · CLIENT HANDOVER ACTION PLAN

From website to live operations

Recommended client action plan

The public site and enquiry flows are in place. The following owner-led confirmations and technical setup will help ensure the experience matches what the Hub can deliver.

High priority: make sponsor enquiries work

The sponsorship form submits to /api/sponsor, but this repository contains no API handler for that route. Implement the endpoint, select the receiving mailbox/service, deploy it and test successful and failed submissions before relying on the form or its two-business-day response message.

Event and registration readiness

  • Confirm the physical venue/address and publish a precise map pin and directions.
  • Approve event dates, schedule, divisions, weight categories, event rules, draw and officials with the organising and technical authorities.
  • Confirm the advertised prizes, eligibility/award conditions, KSh 1,000 fee and verified payment instructions.
  • Agree who responds to athlete/guardian WhatsApp enquiries and how official entries and confirmations are completed.
  • Build separate club/team and officials flows if online submission is required for those groups.

Brand, content and governance

  • Replace the five temporary sponsor-logo spaces only after partner agreements and approved artwork are available.
  • Replace or remove placeholder Facebook, Instagram and X links.
  • Confirm contact phone/email, training times, fees and which non-taekwondo activities are actually available.
  • Review privacy, safeguarding, terms and cookie content, particularly before collecting information from children.
  • Set a named owner for enquiries, website updates, event notices and privacy requests.
