What the website includes

Home · About · Programmes · Register · Tournament · Athlete registration · Sponsors · International pathway · News/gallery · Contact · Privacy, cookies, terms and safeguarding.

Responsive branded pages, mobile navigation, contact shortcuts, page/social metadata, structured data and a sitemap.

Visitor capabilities

  • Explore junior and adult taekwondo, holiday camps and school partnership information.
  • Register interest in Hub activities; minors are prompted for guardian details.
  • Send an athlete entry enquiry for Kyorugi and/or Poomsae with conditional age/category fields.
  • Review event schedule, divisions, awards, spectator information and competition format.
  • Compare sponsorship tiers and download the sponsorship pack.

Form workflow: general and athlete forms prepare WhatsApp messages for the visitor to review and send. Tournament entry fee shown: KSh 1,000 per participant. Registration opens 1 February 2027.

Event & partnerships

Olympic-style Kyorugi: elimination bracket, bout winners advance. Poomsae is score-judged, not an elimination bracket. This does not imply Olympic or federation sanction.

Important operational boundaries

  • Static site: no CMS/admin, database or athlete management system.
  • Forms do not store entries, accept payments or upload documents; WhatsApp enquiry is not confirmed registration.
  • No dedicated club/team or officials online registration flows.
  • Event venue, schedule and technical regulations need final organiser confirmation.
Before handover: Sponsor form submits to /api/sponsor, but this repository has no API handler. Implement and test that service before relying on sponsor enquiries. Replace placeholder social links and temporary sponsor marks after confirmation.

Client follow-up
